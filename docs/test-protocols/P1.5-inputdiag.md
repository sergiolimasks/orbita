# TEST P1.5 — inputdiag (wireless RF probe)

## Objective

Determine whether the console's **internal RF module** — which pairs with original Xbox 360 wireless pads without any external USB receiver — is visible to bare-metal libxenon's OHCI USB stack, and whether libxenon's driver table binds it.

**This is a diagnostic, not a fix.** Its output tells us the next step.

## User actions

1. During the 20-second window: **press each and every button** — A, B, X, Y, LB, RB, LT, RT, both sticks click (L3/R3), D-pad all 4 directions, Start, Back, **the central XBOX button**.
2. Also: press the **power button on the console** briefly (one short tap — does NOT power off, just registers an SMC event).
3. After the 20 s: **photograph the entire screen top-to-bottom**. Multiple photos if it scrolls.

## What matters most in the photo

- The `[USB] addr=... VID=xxxx PID=xxxx class=...` lines — **especially any VID `045e` (Microsoft) entries and whether they say DRIVEN or no-driver**.
- Any line marked `[known but UNBOUND]` or `(unknown VID/PID — NOT in libxenon usbdevs.c)`.
- The `SUMMARY` block at the end (`pad_events=`, `smc_ir_events=`, `DIAGNOSIS`).

## Interpretation cheat-sheet

| What you see | Meaning | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| VID `045e:0719` (or similar Microsoft ID) shows up as `no-driver` / `UNBOUND` | Internal RF module IS on the bus, just not driven. **Best case** — 1-line patch to `usbdevs.c` fixes it. | I add the entry to libxenon, rebuild, redeploy, we retest. |
| VID `045e:028e` DRIVEN + `pad_events > 0` when a WIRED pad is plugged in | usbctrl works, only wireless is broken | Same as above. |
| No `045e` device at all on any bus | Internal RF port not enumerated in bare-metal boot | Options: (a) plug wired pad for dev; (b) get a USB Wireless Receiver dongle; (c) push product to XEX/kernel target for input (ADR-006 outcome (c)). |
| Bunch of unknown VIDs | Something enumerated but we don't know what | Send photo; I look up each VID. |
| `pad_events > 0` immediately | You have a working config already | Update hwtest expectations — was probably a false negative earlier. |
